# Customer Behavior Analysis

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior in a retail business using Python, SQL, and Data Analytics techniques. The objective of this analysis is to generate meaningful business insights that can help improve customer retention, optimize marketing strategies, and increase overall revenue.

---

## Problem Statement
A retail company was facing several business challenges, including:

- Low customer retention
- Lack of product demand insights
- Poor customer segmentation
- Seasonal demand fluctuations
- Limited understanding of customer payment behavior

The goal of this project was to analyze customer transaction data and uncover insights that support better business decision-making.

---

## Business Questions Solved

- Which product category generates the highest revenue?
- Do discounts actually increase purchase value?
- What is the revenue generated by male and female customers?
- Which customers used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount?
- Top & bottom 5 products based on average review ratings
- Average purchase comparison: Standard vs Express shipping
- Do subscribed customers spend more than non-subscribed customers?
- Top 5 products with highest discount usage percentage
- Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal customers
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
- Revenue contribution by different age groups
